# Third-party notices

The scaffold contains no runtime dependency, vendored third-party source, generated
third-party artifact, or imported product implementation.

A derived product must update this file in the same isolated commit that introduces
dependency or imported/generated churn. Record the exact version or revision,
upstream source, license, intended use, integrity/provenance mechanism, redistribution
obligations, and any accepted advisory or compatibility risk.

Do not copy notices from another Audience Foundry product unless the same dependency
and exact version are actually present.
